03 — Process

From script to screen

Four stages, one point of contact, no surprises in between.

Stage 1 — Script

We ask, you answer

A short call and a working brief: who you serve, what they need to do on your site, and what success looks like.

Stage 2 — Draft

Design comes first

You see and approve the full look of the site before a single line of code is written.

Stage 3 — Rehearsal

Build and test

The site gets built, checked on real devices, and tightened until it loads fast and feels right.

Stage 4 — Premiere

Launch day

We publish, connect your domain, and stay on for the first month to fix anything that comes up.
